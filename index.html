About Me

At work

As a product manager at Kissflow, I own core platform capabilities across Forms, Data, and Apps for Kissflow's enterprise low-code platform. I focus on improving enterprise readiness, platform flexibility, scalability, and AI-assisted experiences for complex business workflows and high-volume data operations.

I work closely with enterprise customers and cross-functional stakeholders to shape product strategy, prioritize the roadmap, and drive alignment on execution and rollout decisions.
